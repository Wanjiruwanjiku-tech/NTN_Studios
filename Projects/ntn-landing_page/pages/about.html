About Me

Hi, I'm Natalie Wanjiru, a curious soul, a storyteller and a multi-passionate creative with a background in paramedical sciences and a growing love for web development, programming, design, and emotional wellness.

I graduated with a degree in Paramedical Sciences from Masinde Muliro University and registered with the Kenya Council of Emergency Medical technicians. While I deeply value the service and science of healthcare, my path has been anything but linear. After facing challenges securing traditional roles in the medical field, I leaned into self-discovery, picking up coding, writing African-centered sci-fi stories and seeking to build tools that empower others.

Over time, I have taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, and I have built multiple web pages and meaningful projects, currently focusing on intergrating AI into healthcare to provide seamless access to medical services for all. I have also volunteered in hospitals, and served my community through sports and fire-fighting roles. Each experience has shaped me into someone who listens deeply, adapts quickly and dares to keep showing up.

I believe in thoughtful technology that serves humanity, healing through storytelling and creating safe digital spaces with an African touch. My dream? To build a brand that merges design, development, and imagination, where code meets culture and tech becomes a bridge to dignity and opportunity.
I am still Learning, still growing, and still dreaming. And i am here, building something real.
