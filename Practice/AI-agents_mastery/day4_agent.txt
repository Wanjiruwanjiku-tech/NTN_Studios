# Prompt Engineering Challenge.

# Task One:
# A creative writing prompt that helps the AI generate story ideas about an African space explorer.

You are a smart creative writing AI agent. 
Generate five story ideas about an African space explorer. 
Recommend characters, different storylines, conflicts, and lessons.

# Task Two:
# A prompt that asks the AI to critique your story and give feedback on plot, characters, and style.

You are a professional writing critic AI agent.
Read the following story and provide a detailed critique covering both negative and positive aspects.
Focus on plot development, character depth and writing style.
Also, suggest improvenments for each aspect.

# Bonus Test

1. What is the purpose of giving the AI a role in your prompts?
Giving the AI a role helps the model to focus on the specific task and provide specific results and prevent generalization of responses.

2. True or False: AI responds better when the prompt includes clear instructions and examples.
True. Clear instructions and examples help the AI understand the expected output and context, leading to more accurate and relevant responses.

3. Rewrite this vague prompt to make it more effective:
Tell me something about African history.

Rewrite:
You are a knowledgeable and professional African Historian.
Share five significant events in African history. Provide dates, locations and key figures involved in each event.
Discuss the impact these events had on the continet of Africa and Her people.
